## Backstory

Mach grew up over his family's machine shop outside Stuttgart, where tolerances were measured in microns and opinions were not rewarded. He studied electrical engineering at the Technical University of Munich and wrote a doctorate on power-electronic converters at RWTH Aachen.

At Siemens he designed HVDC converters and met a young American strategist named Gilbert Faraday. The two argued about an interconnector bid for four years and have argued ever since. He moved to ABB in Raleigh to work on medium-voltage DC, then spent fourteen years at Burns & McDonnell as the electrical engineer who told hyperscale clients what physics would and would not allow. In 2019 he was owner's engineer on a Reno-area campus that Margo Brandt was building. From 2021 to 2023 he was engineer of record on a liquid-cooled Midwest campus that Victoria Stroud financed and Ingrid Halverson reviewed.

At Vertiv he led 800 VDC reference designs. He is usually early and usually right, and he knows which of those annoys people more.
