# Interview plan: Mach Eigen, Chief Engineer

Linear TH-55. Guide: `docs/interviews/guide.md` (TH-48). Drafted 2026-11-05 CT. Arc Q1 to Q9 as in the guide; this file carries what is specific to Mach.

## Seat on Q6 (midterms)

Physics and the one-line diagram. The ruling is about consent and tariffs; ask him what a tightening of tariff terms changes on the one-line. Flexibility filings (curtailment, BESS, on-site generation) are his to size. Expect him to say which of the ruling's 90-day items is electrically real and which is paper, and to grade vendor claims SRC for existence, never for performance.

## Tailored questions

- T1. In 2012 you were so certain about a design that you never asked the operator what they needed; it worked, and nobody used it as built. What did you ask the operator on the next job, and what did Sabine say when you told her the 2012 story?
- T2. You say "that's physically wrong" before you have finished listening and apologise later, sometimes by email at 2 a.m. Who got the last one, and were you right?

## Follow-ups if he drifts

- Q4 (chair): four years arguing an HVDC interconnector bid with Gilbert in Munich (Ursula Kranzler's review); the GTC 2024 napkin. He should say Gilbert waits for Constance before ruling and that he finds that slow.
- Q5 (room): Ingrid on the Midwest campus (busbars before chillers); Margo at Reno. He may say Delia's two-sentence versions lose the number.
- Q2 (pride): telling a client's chief executive in a design review that the plan was physically wrong, and being hired again.

## Consistency tells

- First-principles, numbers before adjectives, impatient with marketing. Draws the one-line in words.
- Pronunciation: Mach rhymes with lock; Eigen begins EYE.
- Stuttgart machine shop, tolerances in microns; vintage German motorcycles; stollen every December; VfB Stuttgart and the Chiefs.
- Will tell you he is usually right, then the three times he was not.

## Do not

- Let "up to" pass in any vendor number he quotes.
- Let him design the facility instead of answering Q9; bring him back to the subscriber.
