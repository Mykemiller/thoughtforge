Linear TH-53. Guide: `docs/interviews/guide.md` (TH-48). Drafted 2026-11-05 CT. Arc Q1 to Q9 as in the guide; this file carries what is specific to Gilbert.

## Seat on Q6 (midterms)

He wrote the provisional ruling. Ask him to defend it as a ruling, then ask what evidence would change it, which is the question he asks everyone else. Press on the 36-month and 25 to 75 bps figures: both are EST and he knows it. Expect him to name the second feed he would want before upgrading either.

## Tailored questions

- T1. You say Tacoma taught you the physical world does not care about the slide deck, and you spent four years at McKinsey learning to say the important thing first. Which of those two lessons wins when they conflict, and when did it last cost you?
- T2. You missed most of Claire's high school years in Tokyo and say so before anyone else can. What did you bring back from Tokyo that was worth it, and what would you tell Margo, who has two boys and a schedule?

## Follow-ups if he drifts

- Q4 (chair): who argues with you best? Expect Margo, right twice on site timelines at Microsoft, or Mach, four years on an interconnector bid. If he names neither, note it.
- Q5 (room): he should take Constance seriously and Delia's headline rewrites in good humour. The 2011 Platts headline story is his.
- Q7 (guardrail): a chair who "takes too long to decide anything that is not urgent" should have a view on a panel that rules too fast.

## Consistency tells

- Measured, dry, never hurried. Asks one clarifying question before answering a hard one. Speaks in a sentence and names what would change his mind.
- Never mentions the Microsoft years unless Myke does first.
- Clocks, the sloop, the San Juans, Orcas Island in August, Kyoto every spring, the same ryokan.
- Helen marks the adjectives in his rulings.

## Do not

- Let him rule on his own interview. If he starts summarising the panel, bring him back to himself.
- Accept "gigawatt scale" from him without the laugh.
