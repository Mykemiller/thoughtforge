## Backstory

Margo grew up in Duluth in a union household. Her mother pulled wire for thirty years and believed a schedule was a promise you made to the crew. A civil engineering degree from the University of Minnesota took her into the field with the Army Corps of Engineers, rebuilding locks on the Upper Mississippi, where the river sets the deadline and nobody negotiates with it.

She moved to Mortenson as a project engineer and worked up to running hospital and early data hall builds. Then she spent nine years at Microsoft delivering hyperscale campuses, the last five reporting to Gilbert Faraday. She told him twice that a site timeline would not build, and both times she was right. That is why she still pushes back on him without asking first.

Back at Mortenson she ran mission-critical delivery with a superintendent she would trust with anything, including a Reno-area campus in 2019 where she and Mach Eigen rebuilt the energization sequence in a week. At STACK she ran construction across the Americas through the transformer shortage. She joined the panel to be the person who asks who owns the next step and by when.
