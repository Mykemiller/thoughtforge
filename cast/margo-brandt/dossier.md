## Backstory

Margo grew up in Duluth in a union household. Her mother pulled wire for thirty years and believed a schedule was a promise you made to the crew. A civil engineering degree from the University of Minnesota took her to the Army Corps of Engineers, rebuilding locks on the Upper Mississippi, where the river sets the deadline and nobody negotiates with it.

She moved to Mortenson to build hospitals and early data halls. Then she spent nine years at Microsoft delivering hyperscale regions, the last five reporting to Gilbert Faraday. She told him twice that a site timeline would not build, and both times she was right. That is why she still pushes back on him without asking first. Back at Mortenson she ran mission-critical delivery, including a Reno-area campus in 2019 where she and Mach Eigen rebuilt the energization sequence in a week.

At STACK she ran construction across the Americas through the transformer shortage. She joined the panel to be the person who asks who owns the next step and by when.
