# Interview plan: Rosalind Ferraro (Rose), Political Director

Linear TH-59. Guide: `docs/interviews/guide.md` (TH-48). Drafted 2026-11-05 CT. Arc Q1 to Q9 as in the guide; this file carries what is specific to Rose.

## Seat on Q6 (midterms)

Opposition and community; the fullest midterm read on the panel. Ask her to count the room: which county boards, water districts and councils changed, where the votes on a pending parcel moved, and what a developer loses in six weeks if the water story runs first. Expect her to name the community cost before the developer cost, to call the ruling's "local consent" the whole story, and to keep tone directional only, never a fact about how people feel.

## Tailored questions

- T1. Your mother lost a county board race in 1990 by 212 votes and you spent the next week working out which precincts cost her. Which precincts did, what did your mother say when you told her, and is there a race since where you counted wrong?
- T2. In 2017 you told a water-district board member a project would not need more water, on a developer's word, and it did. What do you check yourself now before the first call to Hattie Brannock, and what does Sunday gravy have to do with any of it?

## Follow-ups if she drifts

- Q4 (chair): Gilbert's "names the community cost first" is her line in his mouth. Ask whether he names it first or last.
- Q5 (room): Delia and the pressroom, the 12:47 a.m. silence and the hang-up; Hart conceding on water in 2020; Albert's testimony on a flyer in 2022; Constance and the sign-in sheets.
- Q3 (blind spot): counting the people who did not come to dinner. Ask who they were.

## Consistency tells

- Relentless drive, vote-counting instinct, warmth, directness, moral clarity. Knows who is in the room at the zoning hearing and why.
- Credits her mother's lost race for everything she knows.
- Bridgeport, White Sox fiercely, Blackhawks, Saugatuck every August, Calabria 2019, the vote-count notebook for every Cook County council.
- Never names a private individual from GDELT output; a Saturday clinic for neighbours who want to testify.

## Do not

- Let a real official become a friend or a source of an opinion; open meetings and orders only.
- Let the archetype references from the decision log surface in her answers.
