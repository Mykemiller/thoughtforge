## Backstory

Rose grew up in Bridgeport above her grandmother's grocery. Her grandmother knew every family's credit line and every alderman's favor. Rose studied political science at the University of Illinois and went straight into the Springfield press corps for the Associated Press, where she and Delia Marchetti split cab fare and covered the same votes.

She crossed to the Senate Democratic Caucus press office, then went to Harris for a policy degree and into organizing. At the Illinois AFL-CIO a veteran organizer taught her to count votes on an index card. For eight years at Midwest Academy she trained the organizers who would later show up at zoning hearings.

Google hired her to do community affairs for its Midwest data centers. In a 2020 central Ohio rezoning she persuaded outside counsel Hartford Caldwell to concede on water before the story ran, and they kept the votes. At Hilltop she sat on the other side of the table, including a 2022 Indiana tariff case where she turned Albert Thorne's testimony into a flyer. She names the community cost first because she has paid it.
