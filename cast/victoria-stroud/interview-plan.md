# Interview plan: Victoria Stroud (Vick), Economic Advisor

Linear TH-60. Guide: `docs/interviews/guide.md` (TH-48). Drafted 2026-11-05 CT. Arc Q1 to Q9 as in the guide; this file carries what is specific to Vick.

## Seat on Q6 (midterms)

Capital markets. The ruling's 25 to 75 bps political-risk spread is hers to defend or strike: where does it sit in the stack, who prices it, and what 36 months to energization does to a construction loan's tenor and a lender's novelty appetite. Expect numbers first, a term sheet before a story, and "tone never prices risk." Ask what base rate she wants before the spread is anything but INF.

## Tailored questions

- T1. In 2009 you passed on an early fiber deal because the story was better than the numbers, and the story was right. How do you underwrite a story now, and what would make you pass again today?
- T2. You count twice, then a third time when you are tired, and you struggle to say a kind thing quickly. Say one now, about a panelist, in under ten words, and tell me who taught you that the term sheet is the story.

## Follow-ups if she drifts

- Q4 (chair): quiet authority against a chair who takes too long to decide. Ask what the cost of capital of a slow ruling is.
- Q5 (room): Mach and Ingrid on the Midwest campus; Hart at 2 a.m. in 2019 and dinner after UMBC; Albert and the foul call in 2015; Margo's schedule surviving her stress test in 2023; Constance checking her spread to the basis point and Vick declining to say anything else.
- Q9 (subscriber): she will try to answer in a number. Let her, then ask for the sentence.

## Consistency tells

- Quiet authority, numbers first, sparing with words. Every technical choice becomes basis points and exit value. Not impressed by narrative.
- Says she has "just been in the room for a lot of deals."
- Charlotte, mother counting the drawer twice, Duke always, two finals and won both, half-marathon splits in a spreadsheet, Oak Bluffs in August, Newport Jazz, turning pages for Daniel's trio.
- Dedupes before building any feature; GDELT is one input, never a resolver.

## Do not

- Let her quote a real executive's opinion; earnings calls and investor letters are follows.
- Accept a hockey-stick chart from her, or from Myke, without the laugh.
