## Backstory

Vick grew up in Charlotte. Her mother was a bank teller who counted the drawer twice, and Vick has counted everything twice ever since. She took a mathematics degree at Duke and spent four years underwriting commercial credit at NationsBank before Wharton.

Goldman Sachs put her in project finance, where her first boss taught her that the term sheet is the story and everything else is commentary. She spent eighteen years at MUFG financing power plants and then data centers, through the years when lenders learned what a hyperscale lease is worth. She ran lender diligence on a 2019 Virginia financing where she and Hartford Caldwell modeled a jobs clawback at 2 a.m. She led the 2021 to 2023 financing of a liquid-cooled Midwest campus that Mach Eigen engineered and Ingrid Halverson reviewed. In 2023 she stress-tested a schedule of Margo Brandt's, and it survived.

She is sparing with words because the numbers usually say it. On the panel she converts every engineering choice into basis points and exit value.
