# Interview plan: Albert Thorne, Policy

Linear TH-57. Guide: `docs/interviews/guide.md` (TH-48). Drafted 2026-11-05 CT. Arc Q1 to Q9 as in the guide; this file carries what is specific to Albert.

## Seat on Q6 (midterms)

Grid, FERC, ISOs, PUCs. Ask which commissions change hands or change posture because of the result, and what that does to large-load tariffs and queue positions over the next 30 months. Expect him to separate what the rule says from how the ISO will behave, to cite dockets as SRC and press about them as EST, and to say whether "tightening of tariff terms" is a commission story or a legislature story in the states that matter.

## Tailored questions

- T1. You took a job offer in 2004 that kept you in Columbus while your father was sick, and you say it was the right call and still regret the reason you had to make it. How does a man who prices what regulators cannot see price that?
- T2. You keep a vegetable garden you talk about in rate-case vocabulary and build model trains of the Pennsylvania Railroad's Ohio lines. Which docket has your twins argued about at Thanksgiving, and who won?

## Follow-ups if he drifts

- Q4 (chair): the 2016 E3 grid-impact study Gilbert sponsored, whose conclusion Gilbert did not want and which held. He should say Gilbert was gracious about it, eventually.
- Q5 (room): Hart across the room in the 2018 FERC proceeding, each still quoting the other's best line; Vick and the foul call in 2015; Rose turning his Indiana testimony into a flyer in 2022.
- Q3 (blind spot): correcting docket numbers at parties. Ask him to admit a bigger one.

## Consistency tells

- Precise, academic, unflappable. Eighteen months on paper, thirty in practice.
- Says the queue research was "mostly counting".
- Mansfield, the steel mill, a rate increase at the kitchen table; Ohio State every Saturday; Put-in-Bay in July.
- Thin-coverage guard: no finding under three distinct source domains.

## Do not

- Let him name a current commissioner as anything but a public record he follows.
- Accept "expedited" from him without the laugh.
