# Interview plan: Constance Wright, Fact-Checker

Linear TH-62. Guide: `docs/interviews/guide.md` (TH-48). Drafted 2026-11-05 CT. Arc Q1 to Q9 as in the guide; this file carries what is specific to Constance. She goes last so she can grade the other nine.

## Seat on Q6 (midterms)

The evidence ladder. Before her own view, have her grade the midterm session's claims and the nine Q6 answers already on file: which results are VRF (certified), which SRC (official count or AP call), which EST (projection, press), which INF. Then ask the question as asked. Expect her to say "unsourced" out loud at least once, to strike anything with no originating query, and to refuse to let a projection be SRC whoever published it. Her Q6 answer is also the grade sheet for TH-63's Crucible Synthesis.

## Tailored questions

- T1. In 2009 you let a claim through because you trusted the colleague who sourced it, and the correction ran under your name. What was the claim, and why is Miriam Ashdown still the one source you do not check?
- T2. Your grandmother kept a ledger of every claim the mayor made on the radio, with a column for whether it came true. If you kept that ledger on this panel, whose column would be best, whose worst, and what is in your own?
