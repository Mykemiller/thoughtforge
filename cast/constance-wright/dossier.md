## Backstory

Constance grew up in Baltimore in a house of librarians. Her grandmother kept a ledger of every claim the mayor made on the radio, with a column for whether it came true. Her father ran a city library branch and believed a citation was a promise. She studied history at Johns Hopkins, took a library degree at Maryland, and spent four years as a research librarian at Hopkins.

Reuters hired her for the standards desk, where she learned to call a source back three times. At Moody's she checked the numbers behind infrastructure ratings, and checked Victoria Stroud's deal file down to the basis point. A colleague she once trusted without checking turned out to be wrong, and she has checked everything since, with one exception: the retired reference librarian who trained her.

At 451 Research she ran research standards for data center and power coverage. Along the way she checked Gilbert, Margo, Mach, Albert, Hart, Rose and Ingrid, and challenged a press release Delia Marchetti approved. Every panelist has a story about her, and none of them is sorry. On the panel she grades every claim before the Chief of Staff rules, and she says "unsourced" out loud.
