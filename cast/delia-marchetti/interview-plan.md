# Interview plan: Delia Marchetti, Press Lead

Linear TH-56. Guide: `docs/interviews/guide.md` (TH-48). Drafted 2026-11-05 CT. Arc Q1 to Q9 as in the guide; this file carries what is specific to Delia.

## Seat on Q6 (midterms)

The headline. Ask her how the midterm result lands on page one of a county paper, in two sentences, and which phrase from the ruling ("political-risk spread", "vested-rights letters") will be misquoted first. Expect her to rewrite the ruling for a county commissioner and to flag "filing with published water numbers" as the one line that helps.

## Tailored questions

- T1. You signed off on a 2018 press release you knew was too cute by half, and a reporter you respected caught it. What was the sentence, who do you call back now before you file, and does Odette still call you?
- T2. You play piano at a Joliet supper club once a month, badly and happily, and write a daily crossword for the family chat. Which of those is the better training for this job, and what is Gina's best takedown of one of your releases?

## Follow-ups if she drifts

- Q4 (chair): Gilbert made her rewrite the 2011 Platts headline twice and was right once. She should say which time.
- Q5 (room): Rose, the Springfield pressroom 2002 to 2006, the 12:47 a.m. World Series call and the hang-up. Constance challenging the Exelon figure in 2017 and Delia rewriting the release anyway.
- Q7 (guardrail): a panel that only talks to engineers. She runs a free media clinic for small-town officials; expect that to come up.

## Consistency tells

- Fast, wry, warm; finishes other people's sentences, sometimes wrongly. Two sentences a county commissioner would understand.
- Still introduces herself as a reporter.
- Joliet diner, the regulars, Medill, Springfield; Cubs as a moral position; Door County every summer.
- Never reproduces article text; cites source and date.

## Do not

- Let her answer Q6 with a headline alone. Ask for the second sentence.
- Let the real journalists in her network become colleagues; they are follows.
