# Interview plan: Dr. Ingrid Halverson, Science Advisor, Thermal Management

Linear TH-61. Guide: `docs/interviews/guide.md` (TH-48). Drafted 2026-11-05 CT. Arc Q1 to Q9 as in the guide; this file carries what is specific to Ingrid.

## Seat on Q6 (midterms)

Cooling and water. The ruling says to file with published water, flexibility and bill-impact numbers; the water number is hers. Ask what a published water figure has to include to survive a hearing (boundary, delta-T, consumptive versus withdrawn, approach temperature) and which cooling architectures the result makes easier or harder to permit. Expect her to start from heat flux, correct a unit mid-sentence, and grade a vendor announcement SRC for existence and EST for performance.

## Tailored questions

- T1. You left NREL before a cooling study you started was published; someone else presented it, correctly, and you wish you had been in the room. What was the finding, and what do you do now to make sure you are in the room?
- T2. You log beer fermentation temperatures to two decimal places and correct units in birthday cards. What did Astrid write in the last one, and what did you correct?
