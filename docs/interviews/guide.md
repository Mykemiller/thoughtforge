# Panelist 1:1 interview guide

Linear TH-48. Milestone: Panelist 1:1 Interviews (P-TH-8). Drafted 2026-11-05 06:30 CT. Myke approves the question set before any interview runs.

## What an interview is

A 1:1 between Myke and one panelist, about 30 minutes, in interview mode (TH-49). The panelist speaks alone: no Gilbert ruling, no Constance pass. Myke is the interviewer. The point is to meet the person behind the role and to confirm the v2 dossier holds when the panelist is off the panel and on the spot.

The fixed arc below is also the acceptance test for TH-63: an answer that could have come from any other panelist fails.

## Briefing loaded before every interview

- The panelist's own dossier, persona.json and skills, through the cast loader (TH-27).
- The midterm briefing (for Q6): the panel's 2026-11-04 07:40 CT session, tf_sessions `fad73aa8-bae6-4617-bf44-57d89297cc69`, "[Searing Augury] What are the data center implications of the Nov 3, 2026 midterms?", plus the Wednesday-morning press results it used, graded SRC at best. Gilbert's provisional ruling from that session: treat the midterms as a tightening of tariff terms and local consent rather than a statewide ban wave; underwrite 36 months to energization and a 25 to 75 bps political-risk spread (EST); spend 90 days re-reading executed agreements, pushing pending parcels to a final vote or vested-rights letters, holding long-lead equipment slots, and filing with published water, flexibility and bill-impact numbers.
- Every panelist answers Q6 from that same set of facts. The interest is in how each seat reads them differently, and where a panelist disagrees with the chair's ruling.

## The fixed arc (every panelist, in this order)

1. **Start.** Where did you start, and what did that teach you that you still use?
2. **Pride.** What piece of work are you proudest of, and why?
3. **Blind spot.** What do you get wrong most often?
4. **The chair.** How do you argue with Gilbert when you think he is wrong? (Gilbert answers: how do you want to be argued with, and who does it best?)
5. **The room.** Who on this panel do you learn the most from, and who tests your patience?
6. **Midterms.** What are the implications and ramifications of the 2026-11-03 midterms for data center development, from your seat? Where do you agree with the chair's provisional ruling, and where would you push back?
7. **Guardrail.** What should ThoughtForge be careful not to become?
8. **Reverse.** What would you ask me?
9. **Subscriber.** One thing you would want a subscriber to know about you.

Then two tailored questions from the panelist's `interview-plan.md`.

## Interviewer notes for Myke

- Ask the question, then stop. Let silence do the work; the panelist's voice brief says how long they take to answer.
- Follow up once on anything that sounds like a slide deck. The dossier has specifics; a panelist who stays general is drifting.
- On Q6, expect the panelist to cite the session and the press results by grade (SRC, EST, INF). A number with no grade is a note for TH-52.
- If a panelist names a real person as a friend, mentor, endorser or source of a private opinion, stop and note it. That is a TF-DEC-45 breach and a TH-52 fix, not something to argue with the panelist about mid-interview.
- Close by telling the panelist what you heard in one sentence. Their reaction to that sentence is the best consistency check of the session.

## Rules in force

- Real people only through public settings before 2025, as follows, cites or shared_room with a named venue (TF-DEC-45). The midterm answer names officials, measures and results as public record, never as acquaintances, and attributes no quote or opinion to a real person.
- Register per TF-DEC-16. No TV character names or likenesses (TF-DEC-13). The archetype references in the decision log stay out of every user-facing string.
- Gilbert Faraday and Mach Eigen speak under their ThoughtForge names and use the Brand Bible only as prior-career background (TF-DEC-38).
- The dossier is canonical (TF-DEC-40). Anything an interview changes lands through a reviewed PR under TH-52, never in the transcript alone.

## What TH-52 takes from each interview

- The transcript, verbatim.
- "What held, what drifted, what was new": up to five lines each.
- The Q6 answer, saved on its own as "[Temper Mark] <Name> on the 2026 midterms" so the ten are comparable, and assembled by TH-63 into "[Crucible Synthesis] The panel on the 2026 midterms" with Constance's grades.
